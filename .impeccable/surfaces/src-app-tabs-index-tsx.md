# Mobile anime shelf

Mode: Operate. Scope: all Android app screens and shared UI.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal anime shelf that makes finding the next episode and scanning a large library effortless.

OWN-WORLD: Aubergine charcoal background, violet tonal surfaces, lilac interactive accents, clean native typography, unaltered anime art, 12–16dp corners, 48dp touch controls. Progress uses a short labeled bar, never decorative gauges.

STORY: The viewer finds a watching title, opens it, sees progress and episodes first, and adjusts trackers or release preferences as needed. Discovery and airing remain distinct destinations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact NekoStream wordmark, large Library title with a quiet count, horizontal Continue watching shelf of poster-and-title rows, status tabs, search and sort, then a compact three-column poster library. Watching cards link directly to anime details; active navigation receives a tonal indicator.

FORM: Anime shelf, grounded candidate 1, chosen over the assigned candidate through the user's decision page; seed 282a15d9. The signature interaction is jumping from the watching shelf straight to the episode controls, with details and feed configuration progressively disclosed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
